Hello! My name is Leticia, and I am passionate about combining technology, data, and creativity to build innovative solutions. My journey began with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at UFRJ, where I explored machine learning, statistical analysis, data visualization, and software development, building a strong foundation in solving complex problems through technology.

Throughout my career in data science and automation, I have worked on projects involving data analysis, process optimization, and the development of intelligent solutions. These experiences shaped my analytical mindset and my ability to transform ideas into practical tools that improve workflows and decision-making.

Today, I am expanding my journey into the audiovisual industry at Globo, where I work with the Special Effects and Automation teams, contributing to the development of technological solutions used in television productions. I participate in the creation and operation of automation systems for major productions, combining engineering, creativity, and production knowledge behind the scenes.

My goal is to continue exploring the intersection between technology and audiovisual, using data, automation, and innovation to enhance creative processes and contribute to the future of entertainment.
